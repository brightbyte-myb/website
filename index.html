How we ship

The journey you just flew through.

Four stages, one continuous flight — the same path every Bytes 360 project takes.

STAGE 01

Discover

A one-to-two-week sprint that turns an idea into a validated scope, an estimate, and a plan.

STAGE 02

Design

Clickable prototypes and system architecture, reviewed together before build begins.

STAGE 03

Build

Weekly cycles with a Friday demo. You watch the product grow in a staging environment from week one.

STAGE 04

Launch & evolve

Monitored launch, handover or ongoing support — your call. Either way, you own all of it.
